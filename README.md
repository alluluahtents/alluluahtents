# AL LULUAH TENTS & SHEDS TR
اللؤلؤة لتجارة الخيام والمظلات

We are a Sharjah-based company that designs, supplies and installs tents, shade structures and outdoor solutions for homes and businesses in the UAE. We help customers choose a layout and finish based on their space and how they plan to use it.

## What We Offer

| Service | Typical uses |
| --- | --- |
| Custom and ready-made tents | Residential outdoor spaces, events and commercial settings |
| Royal tents and traditional Bayt Al Sha'ar tents | Majlis seating, hospitality and private gatherings |
| Car parking shades | Villa driveways and commercial parking areas |
| Outdoor shade structures | Gardens, patios, poolside areas and outdoor seating |
| Aluminium pergolas | Courtyards, terraces and outdoor living areas |
| Outdoor privacy screens | Boundaries, seating areas and spaces that need extra privacy |
| Tent interiors and seating | Interior finishes, majlis layouts and sofa arrangements |
| Hangars and warehouse structures | Covered spaces for storage and commercial use |

## Request a Quote

Send us your project location, the type of structure you need, approximate dimensions and photos of the area if available. For parking shades, include the number of parking spaces. For tents or majlis areas, tell us how you plan to use the space and how many people it should accommodate.

Visit the [official AL LULUAH website](https://alluluh.com/) to browse our services and contact our team.
